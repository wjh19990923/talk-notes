为什么一家银行给它的 AI 分析师配了一台计算器

Langfuse 的 Doneyli De Jesus 介绍了一家排名前五的银行如何构建上线关卡，区分“找到了正确金融数据的智能体”和“给了银行家正确数字的智能体”。

01

为等待财报电话会的投资银行家打造的基准

When an earnings call ends, an investment banker wants to know what an analyst at the firm makes of it. Getting a summary of the transcript quickly would accomplish part of the job. An experienced analyst also brings knowledge of the firm and how it operates to the reading. The bank wanted its proposed 10-K analyst to bring that context to bear while the information was still fresh. How could the people responsible for the application tell whether it had done that work well enough to put the result in a banker's hands?

Doneyli De Jesus leads AI engineering solutions at Langfuse by ClickHouse, an open-source platform for agent observability and evaluation. His team helped a top-five bank work out how to make release decisions like this one. The public benchmarks that accompany a new model could tell the engineers about its technical capabilities, sometimes across many dimensions. But the bank had its own datasets, ways of building models and uses for their output. It considered much of that work proprietary. A performance gain on an open dataset gave the bank little basis for estimating the gain an investment banker would see.

The analyst posed two separate engineering problems. The team had to make the firm's knowledge available to a model that had little reason to know it out of the box. Then it had to establish whether the resulting analysis was correct. As De Jesus recalled the bank's rule, “We don't certify models here. We certify applications.” A 10-K analyst and an investment analysis application could use the same underlying model and still call for different evidence at release time.

02

关于评测能在哪里运行的三个约束

The first requirement De Jesus encountered in finance was on-premises operation. After years of moving work to the cloud, he had seen banks reconsider data centers they already had. The sensitivity of the material going into models made it worthwhile for some to repurpose those facilities and stand up GPU infrastructure for local inference. Some banks even had research divisions where people with PhDs trained and fine-tuned their own models. An evaluation method had to be applicable to whatever model the institution could run there.

The second requirement was gated network access. The bank wanted control over where its data went, even when a model provider guaranteed zero data retention. Even the appearance that a provider might train on the data mattered. The third requirement was an air-gapped deployment that kept the system isolated from external networks. Within the bank’s infrastructure, De Jesus emphasized that only people who needed the data should see it. That mattered for evaluation, too, because an agent’s trace could contain the internal material it retrieved.

03

让每次配置改动都能用可重复的测试来检验

Once the platform and models were in place, the bank still had three things to prove. The application had to perform the intended task, satisfy the bank's compliance requirements and produce something people could use. The third condition had an organizational consequence. Bankers who kept getting unusable analyses would stop using the application, and the experience could sour them on other AI-enabled work. The team wanted a pass-or-fail decision it could revisit whenever someone edited the system prompt, changed the model or gave the agent another tool.

De Jesus started with a golden dataset. In software engineering terms, its entries were the application's test cases. In his experience, banks often already had a set of examples they trusted. What they lacked was a way to exercise those examples against each candidate configuration. Keeping the questions fixed lets engineers return to a case after a change and examine what happened to the answer.

Running the agent on those cases produces traces. Each trace records evidence of what the team evaluated and how the agent handled it. A dataset entry can establish what the bank expected. The corresponding trace can show, for example, which tool the agent actually called on the way to its answer. Evaluators use that evidence to score the run along the dimensions the bank selects. Only then does the team have something to compare with a release threshold.

04

小模型能找到数字，却仍可能算错

The team tried models of different sizes and capabilities. One small model earned 97% on groundedness. Here, groundedness measured how well it extracted information from internal sources and based its response on data the bank actually held. That was a passing result. If the team had investigated only whether the analyst could find and use the bank's material, it would have had little reason to change this part of the application.

The same model scored 60% on numerical accuracy. The difference showed up when a financial answer required arithmetic. An answer could draw on the appropriate source and still report the wrong figure, even for a simple calculation. De Jesus attributed that weakness to asking a next-token predictor to do math. For the team, the distinction narrowed the problem considerably. The evidence pointed to the work the agent did with the retrieved information, after it had found the material it needed.

05

在计算步骤接入工具，在工作流之后设置关卡

De Jesus followed a test case from the golden dataset into the agent. The agent planned the task, retrieved data from official sources and reached a calculation step. That last step was where the team intervened. Arithmetic with given inputs is deterministic, so the team gave the agent a calculator it could call. The agent still had to work out what the question required and retrieve the relevant information. The calculator took over the operation that had been yielding unreliable numbers.

The gate sat at the end of that workflow. Numerical accuracy was one condition the application had to clear. Other dimensions, including regulatory compliance, could have thresholds of their own. A change that improved arithmetic therefore had to go through evaluation of the whole application. Even an average above 90% could leave a candidate below a required threshold in one category. That candidate would be held back from production.

This arrangement also gave the bank a way to consider a future model swap. The new model could run inside the agent with the same task, sources and tools. The bank could then apply the same quality requirements to what came out. Approval of the previous configuration supplied a basis for comparison. The new configuration would earn its own result.

06

把预期答案、计算器调用和实测收益联系起来

The example dataset had three elements per case: an input, an expected output and accompanying metadata. Together these gave the team a reference for assessing the agent's response and behavior. De Jesus then showed a scored agent run and singled out the calculate tool call in its trace. The dataset supplied the expectation before the run. The call was evidence from execution that the agent had used the capability the engineers introduced.

The instruction was to use the small piece of Python code whenever a number needed calculating. With that tool available, numerical accuracy rose from 60% to roughly 95%. The distinction between the tool and the measured result matters here. Python can calculate deterministically once it receives the inputs. The roughly 95% figure described how often the application got the numerical answer right on the evaluation. The improvement included the agent's ability to get from a question to a calculation and return the result to the user.

07

用代码、路径检查和 LLM 评判者回答不同的问题

De Jesus described three ways to score a run. The first was deterministic scoring in code. For the same inputs, the scorer produces the same output each time. A numerical result with a known expected value is a natural fit. The Python calculator and a deterministic scorer have different jobs here. One helps produce the answer during execution. The other assesses the result, so engineers can tell whether adding the tool improved performance across cases.

The second was a path check. An answer can look complete even when the agent skipped a required step. Inspecting the trace lets an evaluator ask whether the agent took the appropriate route. In this example, the presence of a calculate call answers a different question from whether the final figure was correct. An agent might happen to supply the right number on a case where it did the arithmetic itself. A check of the answer alone would miss that it bypassed the tool.

The third was an LLM judge for more qualitative aspects of the response. De Jesus favored a blend as applications became more complex. Code can settle reproducible questions, the path exposes missing actions, and a judge can assess qualities of an answer that are harder to express in code. Together those results gave the bank evidence it could tie to an identified change in a system prompt, model or set of tools. In the calculator example, the small model's application went from failing numerical accuracy to clearing the 85% gate for that use case.

08

由风控团队决定哪个分数可以阻止发布

Selecting an evaluator still leaves a decision about what score is good enough. For an AI-enabled risk application, De Jesus wanted the people responsible for risk involved in setting that bar. They could judge what they would be comfortable putting in front of colleagues or customers. The order mattered. First identify the dimensions on which this particular application should be judged. Then set a threshold for each. The analyst's 85% gate offered no general answer to what another use case should require.

De Jesus described full runs on two datasets, one with about 150 questions and another with about 1,500. He illustrated the release decision with a configuration that passed on groundedness, completeness and tool use but scored zero on regulatory compliance. Compliance in this example was binary, scored at zero or 100. A configuration could therefore retrieve the right material, carry out the expected steps and supply a complete answer, yet fail a condition the bank considered binding.

Averaging across dimensions could let high scores for groundedness, completeness and tool use outweigh the compliance failure. The bank could still face a penalty or reputational damage for releasing that configuration. With a separate compliance gate, a zero blocked promotion even when the other scores passed.

09

把失败的 trace 交给专家，保留值得反复测试的用例

De Jesus described an annotation queue with a specific selection rule. Whenever regulatory compliance failed, the team could send the corresponding trace to a domain expert. That gave the reviewer the question, the answer and the steps behind it. The expert could inspect what happened and give engineers feedback on a particular failure, without having to find it among hundreds of successful cases. Engineers could use that feedback to improve the agent.

Two events then fed different parts of the process. Promoting a prompt or changing the configuration triggered another run through the gates. Expert feedback, meanwhile, made a reviewed case a candidate for the golden dataset. An expert might identify a better expected result for the dimension that failed. If the team included that case, the next candidate would face a test informed by an actual failure and someone qualified to judge it.

10

这家银行几分钟内就能复现的东西

De Jesus reported that the bank reduced the time it took to evaluate AI applications from weeks to minutes. He singled out risk and compliance as areas where evaluation can sometimes take months. With cases, scorers and thresholds in place, the team could rerun a changed configuration and focus attention on the dimensions that failed. The reported time saving applied to evaluation. Experts still had to establish what good work looked like and review failures that warranted their judgment.

De Jesus closed by pointing practitioners to a detailed account of the work and a repository for reproducing it. What another team could reproduce was the process. Its own data would determine the benchmark, the application would determine which behaviors to measure, and the people accountable for its use would decide which results justified a release.

要点总结

  • 从机构已经信任的案例开始。把每个输入与其预期输出配对，并在候选配置运行时保留 trace。
  • 分别给检索、算术和工具使用打分。该银行 97% 的有据可依分数掩盖了仅 60% 的数值准确率，而计算调用给了工程师一个可验证的具体干预点。
  • 让领域专家按用例设定阈值。给一票否决的合规失败单独设一道发布关卡，并把失败的 trace 交给能评估它的人。
  • 用审查过的失败改进黄金数据集，然后在提示词、模型或工具变化时重新运行它。
