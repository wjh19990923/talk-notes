提示词工程与 AI 红队测试

一次务实的剖析：提示词为什么会成功或失败，自动化优化如何改变评测，以及为什么当模型能够采取行动时，注入攻击会变得危险得多。

Two related strategies aggregate several attempts:

- Ensembling: Give multiple model instances the question, optionally varying roles or tool access, then choose the most common answer.
