指标

幻灯片原文
UMBRELA evaluates retrieval without golden chunks, while AutoNuggetizer evaluates generation without golden answers. Two other metrics check citation correctness and whether responses are consistent with the chunks.

The Metrics

• UMBRELA: retrieval evaluation without “golden chunks”
• AutoNuggetizer: generation evaluation without “golden answers”
• Citation Faithfulness: are the citations in the response really correct?
• Hallucination detection: is the response factually consistent with the chunks?

UMBRELA（检索）

幻灯片原文
UMBRELA scores passages from 0 (unrelated) to 3 (dedicated to the query and containing the exact answer). Intermediate scores cover related or partially useful passages. The speaker says research found a correlation with human judgments.

[2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1cQlnfwmIdU&t=126s) [UMBRELA rates passage relevance from zero to three, with descriptions of each score.](https://assets.ai.engineer/sites/aiecode2025/sha256/16c13f6adf961bece4c803bf80b9bba5c4ff2ce9f6ca16cb8d42f11c0f551423.webp)

UMBRELA (Retrieval)

• On a scale of 0-3 measures how relevant each passage is to the query.
• Research shows UMBRELA correlates well with human judgments.

0 = represent that the passage has nothing to do with the query

1 = represents that the passage seems related to the query but does not answer it,

2 = represents that the passage has some answer for the query, but the answer may be a bit unclear, or hidden amongst extraneous information and

3 = represents that the passage is dedicated to the query and contains the exact answer.

AutoNuggetizer（生成）

幻灯片原文
AutoNuggetizer turns retrieved passages into rated atomic facts, selects the top 20 vital nuggets, then asks an LLM judge whether the RAG answer fully or partially supports each one.

[2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1cQlnfwmIdU&t=171s) [Three steps for creating and rating nuggets, selecting vital nuggets and assessing their support in a RAG response.](https://assets.ai.engineer/sites/aiecode2025/sha256/f5f69b09c545241f53a6a2c9d7e77ee0f6b24644ae8da0961e22e481e00c2211.webp)

AutoNuggetizer (Generation)

• Step 1: Nugget Creation -
  a. An LLM judge is asked to create a set of ‘Nuggets’ of information based on retrieved passages. These are ‘atomic facts’ that should appear in your responses.
  b. Each nugget gets assigned a ‘vital’ or ‘okay’ rating depending on whether it must absolutely be present in the generated response to the query or whether it’s good to have but not absolutely necessary.

• Step 2: Top 20 vital nuggets are selected from Step 1.

• Step 3: LLM judge analyzes the RAG response to determine if each of the selected Nuggets is fully supported or partially supported by the answer.

引用忠实度

幻灯片原文
An LLM judge compares each cited sentence in a RAG response with its cited passage, then labels the support as full, partial, or absent.

[3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1cQlnfwmIdU&t=209s) [An LLM judge compares cited sentences with their cited passages and labels support as full, partial, or absent.](https://assets.ai.engineer/sites/aiecode2025/sha256/85588a5b375860896153f4eaf9135ce575f436d1ceea9ce45ecd35a785c315a0.webp)

Citation Faithfulness

• Generated RAG response is broken down into sentences that have a citation, then each sentence is compared by the LLM judge to the passage that the citation claims it came from.
• Labels: Full Support, Partial Support, or No Support
• Focused on citation-to-passage fidelity

Open RAG Eval：无需标准答案即可评估检索与回答

Open RAG Eval 会采集流水线检索到的段落和生成的答案，然后在没有人工编写标准参考答案的情况下，评估相关性、信息覆盖度、引用支持和回答一致性。

熟悉 RAG（检索段落并用它们生成答案）会有帮助。

01

标准参考答案的瓶颈

How do you evaluate a RAG system when you have useful questions but no curated set of correct answers or ideal retrieved passages? Creating those references adds work every time the evaluation set grows. Open RAG Eval, introduced here by Ofer Mendelevitch of Vectara developer relations, addresses that bottleneck: evaluate retrieval and generation without manually supplied golden chunks or golden answers. The opening slide’s QR code provides access to the repository.

The project draws on research conducted with the University of Waterloo’s Jimmy Lin Lab. Its approach replaces the requirement for curated references with automated assessments of what the pipeline actually retrieves and generates. Understanding those assessments matters: each measures a different part of the RAG system.

02

从查询到评测文件

Start with 10, 100, or 1,000 queries that matter to your RAG application. These are example evaluation-set sizes, not throughput claims. The workflow then follows the data through three stages:

1. Capture the pipeline’s behavior. A RAG connector collects the actual retrieved chunks and generated answers. The demonstrated integrations include Vectara, LangChain, and LlamaIndex. 2. Evaluate the captured outputs. The resulting RAG outputs feed metrics grouped into evaluators. This separates collecting a pipeline’s behavior from assessing it. 3. Write evaluation files. Evaluators emit files containing the results for inspection and comparison.

The connector is therefore the boundary between your application and the evaluation machinery: it supplies what the system did, without requiring you to author the ideal answer first.

03

用 UMBRELA 评估检索相关性

The package introduces four complementary assessments: UMBRELA for retrieval, AutoNuggetizer for generation, citation faithfulness, and hallucination detection using a Vectara model. Keeping these dimensions separate helps distinguish retrieving useful evidence from producing a well-supported answer.

UMBRELA scores individual passages against the query, without requiring golden chunks. Its relevance scale runs from 0 to 3:

| Score | Meaning described in the talk | | --- | --- | | 0 | Passage has nothing to do with the query | | 3 | Passage is dedicated to the query and contains the exact answer |

The endpoints distinguish irrelevant retrieval from a passage that directly supplies the requested information.

Mendelevitch reports that UMBRELA correlates well with human judgment, citing the Waterloo research. That supports using automated relevance assessment without golden chunks, but the consequential distinction is between agreement in evaluating systems and identical labels for every passage. The associated research supports system-ranking agreement; the talk supplies no correlation coefficient or experimental conditions for its broader qualitative claim.

04

用 AutoNuggetizer 评估答案覆盖度

AutoNuggetizer evaluates generation by breaking the information an answer should cover into nuggets, or atomic information units. Instead of comparing the generated response with a single golden answer, it checks coverage of selected units:

1. Create nuggets. Produce atomic units of information for the evaluation. 2. Prioritize them. Assign each nugget a vital or okay importance rating, order the nuggets, and select the top 20. 3. Judge answer coverage. An LLM judge examines the RAG response to determine whether each selected nugget is fully or partially supported by the answer.

The top-20 limit is a selection setting, not an accuracy result. The mechanism measures information coverage: an answer can cover one important point completely and another only partly.

For a small Python implementation of the importance-ordering step, retain each nugget’s text and rating together, then select the first 20 after sorting. This helper represents selection only; nugget creation and support judgments remain separate operations.

```python from typing import Literal, TypedDict

class Nugget(TypedDict): text: str importance: Literal["vital", "okay"]

def select_nuggets(nuggets: list[Nugget]) -> list[Nugget]: priority = {"vital": 0, "okay": 1} ordered = sorted( nuggets, key=lambda nugget: priority[nugget["importance"]], ) return ordered[:20] ```

The original nugget records survive the selection, so the subsequent judge can assess each retained unit against the generated answer.

05

引用支持与整体回答的一致性

Mendelevitch points to the papers for fuller method details before moving to two support checks. These operate at different scopes:

| Assessment | Scope | Question | | --- | --- | --- | | Citation faithfulness | A citation and its associated claim | Does the cited passage support the claim? | | Hallucination detection | The entire response | Does the response align with retrieved content? |

Citation faithfulness distinguishes full support, partial support, and no support. A citation’s presence alone does not establish that its passage substantiates what the answer says.

For the response-level check, HHEM, Vectara’s hallucination detection model, assesses alignment between the answer and retrieved evidence. Here, support is relative to that evidence: a statement can be true elsewhere yet unsupported by the passages supplied to the model. This complements nugget coverage by asking whether the answer stays grounded in what was retrieved.

06

检查结果

Once evaluation finishes, you can inspect the result files directly. For easier comparison, the talk shows a hosted interface and describes dragging the evaluation files onto open-evaluation.ai. The populated view lists queries alongside retrieval and generation scores, with groups of colored results arranged side by side. This makes the separate evaluation dimensions visible together without requiring manual inspection of every file.

07

调优流水线并扩展它的连接器

The intended use is to optimize and tune a RAG pipeline while keeping its evaluation methods inspectable. Open source provides access to the metric implementations, so a score need not remain an opaque judgment: you can examine how the assessment works.

Vectara, LangChain, and LlamaIndex provide the demonstrated integration paths. For a custom pipeline, the extension point is the connector that captures retrieved chunks and generated answers. Mendelevitch invites issues and pull requests for additional connectors, allowing other pipelines to feed the same evaluator workflow.
