“拦截率”在对你撒谎：像衡量员工一样衡量 AI 智能体

Druid AI 借一次银行业务转人工的例子说明，单纯处理更多对话可能意味着把工作做得更差，然后提出了一套涵盖职责授权、治理、运行时行为和业务成果的计分卡。

01

更常向上反映问题的实习生，可能是做得更好的那一位

Two interns join the same banking customer-service team with the same job description. By August, Intern A handles 80% of conversations without a human and escalates 20%; Intern B handles 96% alone and escalates only 4%. With one return offer available, the dashboard makes B look like the obvious hire. Michael, Druid AI’s Head of Developer Community, opens with this choice to expose a problem customers keep asking about: which measurements actually tell you whether an agent is effective?

Consider one of A’s conversations. At 7 a.m., an authenticated customer opens the bank’s mobile app and asks what it would take to pay off a mortgage early. The agent can retrieve the balance, calculate the payoff and give a complete answer. By the usual definition, the conversation could be contained: no human needs to finish the calculation.

But the question also signals that the customer is reconsidering the mortgage—a possible refinancing opportunity. The bank therefore writes a rule: answer the question, then pass the context to a senior banker. This deliberate handoff contributes to A’s 20% escalation rate. Containment, the percentage of conversations handled without a human, counts a useful business decision against the agent. It measures independence while leaving the purpose of the handoff out of view.

02

为存在什么、运行什么、持续受管理的是什么打分

Dan Bălăceanu, Druid AI’s co-founder and Chief Product Officer, introduces the replacement: six pillars divided between configuration and runtime, with governance spanning both to make seven pillars overall. Configuration covers what exists before execution. Runtime covers the tasks an agent creates, the tasks it executes and the output it produces. Governance asks whether that behavior stays within the business’s expected limits. Together, the framework evaluates 26 components.

Each component receives one of three scores:

- 0 — Missing. The component does not exist. - 1 — Observable and measurable. Someone can inspect and measure it, even manually. A written definition of risk is an example: a reviewer can read it and understand what the agent is meant to operate within. - 2 — Managed over time. The component is tracked, observed and analyzed repeatedly. Yesterday’s acceptable behavior does not establish today’s performance.

The distinction rewards ongoing management rather than merely having a definition or collecting a measurement once.

How much should each part influence the result? The presented weighting gives governance 39%, mandate 26%, platform 12% and output 9%. Technical capabilities contribute, but their weights are lower because this scorecard prioritizes business effectiveness. Those weights encode a judgment about importance; the resulting percentage should be read as a structured assessment under that judgment, rather than a universal measure of agent quality.

03

在选择自动化之前先设定容错度

Governance comes first because it can change which work should be automated at all. Bălăceanu describes departments competing to adopt AI after a board directive. Starting with business limits gives them a way to choose a functional area before investing in its technical implementation, rather than discovering the business consequences afterward.

Error tolerance depends on who makes the final decision. An autonomous agent needs a stricter target because no human stands between its output and action; Bălăceanu’s preference is that it meet the requirement or cancel the job. A system drafting content for a human can tolerate more errors because the human still decides what to use. The illustrative percentages in this discussion do not define a reusable acceptance threshold. The useful decision is to set tolerance in relation to the agent’s authority and the human review that remains.

The governance review then examines several distinct kinds of evidence:

- Risk and compliance. Risks should be written down and coordinated with the relevant departments. The example also evaluates compliance against SOC 2 and organizational policies. - Observability. Logs capture inputs, recorded reasoning, decisions and integration calls so reviewers can inspect what happened. - Drift. Behavior is compared with the limits and baseline established at the first go-live, then tracked over time. - Accuracy and hallucination. The example reports a 3% hallucination rate, identified through a combination of automated detection and manual annotation. That is the example’s measurement, not a general rate for agents. - Tone and sentiment. Conversational behavior is assessed alongside factual and operational behavior.

These checks answer different questions. An agent can have extensive logs while still producing inaccurate answers, or remain accurate while drifting outside its intended operating limits.

04

职责授权远不止系统提示词

The agent’s mandate defines the job it is being evaluated against. A system prompt contributes to that definition, but the mandate also needs a business purpose, an allowed scope and operating limits. Without a bounded job, the agent can wander into work it was never meant to perform, making both hallucination control and performance assessment harder.

The concrete requirements cover several parallel concerns:

- Business outcome. A target such as 70% contract renewal states what the agent is meant to achieve and gives the eventual evaluation a business objective. - Evidence and policies. Specify which documents and connected systems supply the facts the agent may reason from. - Access in both directions. Define which systems the agent can use and which people can engage with it. Bălăceanu contrasts allowing three endpoints with exposing an MCP server containing 4,000 tools whose use is poorly understood. - Budget. Set spending limits before deployment rather than discovering a week later that resource use has reached 20K.

The endpoint example makes the security decision tangible: tool availability should follow the job’s requirements, rather than expanding simply because more tools can be connected.

05

共享转人工服务，检查技能被如何使用

Platform capabilities make good behavior reusable across agents. If one agent manages memory, human handoff and skill orchestration well, the organization should be able to use those capabilities elsewhere. The scorecard therefore evaluates whether each agent participates in the shared platform, rather than rebuilding these functions independently.

Human handoff is the clearest example. Bălăceanu proposes a central action center where people engage with agents across the organization. Each agent is assessed on whether it connects to that service. This places human engagement in a common organizational channel instead of leaving every agent to invent its own handoff mechanism.

Skills and tools need a more detailed review than counting them. The questions include how granular and discoverable skills are, how they are orchestrated, how large the prompt combining them becomes, and whether skills come from a central repository, are created during execution or are human-coded. Tool assessment asks whether the agent uses its available tools effectively. A larger inventory alone says little about whether the agent performs its assigned job.

06

把成功标准挂到具体的任务上

At runtime, the mandate becomes tasks that the agent creates and executes. Evaluation needs a record of those tasks, their inputs and the context required to perform them. Otherwise, a reviewer sees an outcome without enough information to judge how it was produced or whether the agent had what it needed.

The contract-renewal example adds an essential link: create success criteria together with the task and associate them with the individual contract. Seeing that a contract was renewed does not by itself answer whether that renewal met the task’s requirements. Criteria attached to that particular task make the result assessable. The agent’s explanation of its actions and the final output validation complete that assessment.

07

完整的评分颠覆了按拦截率的排名

With the components combined, Agent A receives an 83% score and wins the comparison despite its lower 80% containment. Governance, mandate and final outcome change the hiring decision that the opening dashboard appeared to settle.

Which capabilities explain the reversal? In the presented cluster comparison, A leads everywhere except skills. B has more skills, but falls short on explainability, tracking success and complying with governance. The distinction is between possessing capabilities and using them in a way the organization can inspect and evaluate. Druid reports using the framework with multiple customers to give technical teams and business stakeholders a shared basis for that discussion.

08

一个全绿的仪表盘可能掩盖一个下滑的分数

Michael returns to the intern analogy with a useful break in the comparison: “when you hire an agent, it never ends.” The proposed practice is to rerun evaluation weekly on live traffic against a human baseline, then use the observations to improve and retest the agent. This makes drift an operating concern throughout deployment.

What can repeated scoring reveal that the familiar dashboard misses? The presented chart holds a green dashboard metric at 92% all year while the more complete score falls from 83% to 60%. The relationship matters more than either endpoint: a stable measure of apparent performance can coexist with deterioration in the broader assessment. Weekly measurement reveals the decline; it does not cause it.

09

统计银行员工能用释放出来的时间做什么

The ROI question often arrives as a question about whose job the agent replaces. Druid’s proposed alternative is to measure what routine work the agent removes and what that allows people to do next. A useful scorecard needs both sides: work transferred to the agent and valuable work enabled for the human.

The banking example now has a second payoff. The agent handles routine balance checks, leaving the banker time to speak with the customer reconsidering a mortgage. Answering the original question and routing its context support the same business purpose. “The agent made the banker better at banking” is the intended outcome. This is a way to frame ROI; the example supplies neither a monetary return calculation nor a measured revenue uplift.

10

拦截率目标取决于具体的工作

The ending reveals where the opening 80/20 split came from: Druid’s 2026 AI Adoption Benchmark for financial services. Michael describes the benchmark as anonymized aggregate production data collected over 15 months. Its reported containment rates differ substantially by industry:

- Financial services: 80%. - Healthcare: 87%. - HR and IT: 93%. - Higher education: 99%.

These are Druid’s reported industry figures; the talk does not provide sample sizes or enough methodology to turn them into universal performance targets.

Higher education’s 99% does not establish that its agents do a better job than banking’s 80%. Michael attributes banking’s lower figure to more deliberate handoffs in the design. That returns the comparison to the mortgage customer: the right amount of escalation follows the work and its intended outcome. Define the mandate and limits, score the whole agent, and repeat the assessment weekly against a human baseline. Containment becomes one measurement inside that job description.

要点总结

  • 一次有意为之的转接可能降低拦截率，却对业务有利：房贷智能体回答了客户，然后把一个可能的再融资机会转给了银行顾问。
  • 这张记分卡区分了缺失的组成部分、可衡量的组成部分和需要长期管理的组成部分；治理和授权占的权重最大。
  • 在执行之前定义范围、业务目标、允许使用的证据、访问权限和预算，再给每个运行时任务附上成功标准。
  • 反复对照人工基线进行评估，并把自动化腾出来的有价值的人工工作也算进去；一个稳定的拦截率指标可能掩盖总体得分的下降。
  • Beating RL With Reflection: GEPA and Optimize Anything —— 展开了评估中改进的一面：执行轨迹和详细反馈指导对提示词和智能体程序的修改，人工标注则可以塑造评估器。
