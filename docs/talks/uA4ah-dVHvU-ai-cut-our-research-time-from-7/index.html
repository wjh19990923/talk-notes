Data Migration

• Set up a Change Data Capture (CDC) process.
• Use ETL jobs to scan both databases and produce daily audit reports.

Producer Migration

Help me map ledger status codes from DB2 to the Cloud
• Source: migration FAQ doc, confirmed against the Cloud ledger-status enum and the legacy status/activity codes.
• Key insight: two legacy fields collapse into one cloud field.
• DB2 tracks status at two levels — LEDGER_STATUS_CD (coarse: PNDG / SENT / CMPL / REJC / CNCL) and LEDGER_ACTIVITY_CD (fine-grained).
• The Cloud enum has only one field, and it maps from the fine-grained activity code — not the coarse status.

OPEN | n/a | n/a | No | Written to the cloud ledger store, not yet confirmed in DB2
CREATED | ENTR / WEBN / BTCH | PNDG | Yes | Entered, passed validation, not yet sent to recordkeeper
ROUTED_TO_REVIEW | RVRT | PNDG | Yes | Queued for review
APPROVED_BY_REVIEW | APRV | PNDG | No | Reviewed & released, ready to send
REJECTED_BY_REVIEW | REJB | REJC | Yes | Reviewed & rejected
SENT_TO_RECORDKEEPER | SNRK | SENT | No | Sent to the recordkeeper
PROCESSED_BY_RECORDKEEPER | PRCK / PRCM | SENT | No | Posted to the recordkeeper's customer ledger
COMPLETE | CMPL | CMPL | No | In transaction history
CANCELLED | CNCL | CNCL | No | Canceled before completion

AI 把我们的研究时间从 7 天缩短到 15 分钟

Gareth Yoder 讲解了先锋领航（Vanguard）如何通过并行的数据、生产方和消费方迁移，对一个现金账本系统进行现代化改造——并把遗留代码和迁移指导开放给 AI 工具使用。

Yoder 报告称，研究反馈周期从七天缩短到了 15 分钟。

01

一本在持续运转中进行现代化改造的账本

Vanguard’s cash ledger tracks money moving into brokerage accounts and between accounts, across payment rails such as ACH and wires. It serves web, mobile, and batch channels in an active-active, multi-region system. Gareth Yoder, the cash program’s technical lead, says its services receive up to 30 million calls a day and clients generate half a million ledgers daily.

After the 2022 launch of Cash Plus, the program took on modernization as an enterprise priority. Data, producers that write it, and consumers that read it all had to migrate in parallel. That made understanding the existing system an immediate dependency: engineers and architects needed to work out what the new data schema should contain.

Initially, answering a legacy-code question meant asking a mainframe engineer to investigate and return with an analysis. Once the codebase became available to AI tools through an MCP server, the team could prompt the tool directly. Yoder reports that a research feedback loop fell from seven days to 15 minutes. The comparison describes the team’s experience with that research loop; it does not establish that every answer or migration task took 15 minutes.

02

迁移数据并核对细节

The data migration used change data capture (CDC), followed by daily scans of the legacy and cloud databases. The team compared row counts, then checked individual attributes: amounts, account information, and state and federal withholding. The distinction matters: matching counts can reveal missing or extra rows, while column comparisons can reveal records whose contents differ.

Yoder cautions against over-engineering the audit solution. Its job was to support the transition, and the code would be deprecated when modernization was complete. The checks provided migration evidence; matching counts alone would not establish that every value matched.

03

让写入可见，并给生产方一条迁移路径

How did the old write paths compare with the new one? Legacy producers used a service request, an API wrapping that request, or direct table inserts. Direct inserts were particularly troublesome: data appeared in the database without the team necessarily knowing who had produced it. The new design placed a common data schema on a gateway wrapping the APIs that insert cloud data, giving producers a defined route into the modern system.

The move also let the team change behavior. It adopted idempotency—the property that a repeated operation can be handled without creating a second effect—and sought strong consistency across multiple writers. Yoder describes using a paradigm similar to a Snowflake ID, though he does not specify its implementation. The goal was to redesign the producer offering around modern capabilities rather than reproduce every legacy path.

There were 35 producers to bring across. The team first wrote migration documentation manually, published it in the company wiki, and later exposed it through MCP. Producers could then ask questions through prompts instead of relying as heavily on separate support chats. Yoder reports a large reduction in those chats and support requests.

The cloud ledger also used human-readable enum values where the legacy system used status codes. Yoder says the AI tools helped bridge the intent between the old and new representations. The migration guidance was therefore part of the product during onboarding, even though it too would eventually be retired.

04

消费账本的三种方式

Consumers had different timing and data needs, so the modern system offered three access patterns with a schema registry across the channels:

- Real time: The heavily used funds-available API helps determine the balance displayed on an account. Consumers call through GraphQL and select the attributes their use cases need. - Batch: For reconciliation or analytics, the data warehouse exposes data-share views tailored to each consumer’s stated needs. Data governance supported limiting each consumer to the attributes it needs. - Streaming: A Kafka topic carries database inserts and updates. Fraud teams can ingest those events into their tooling in real time.

The event stream provides at-least-once delivery: a consumer can receive an event more than once. That makes idempotent handling important when work is retried. Yoder gives the example of a batch job failing halfway through; a retry needs to avoid creating duplicate ledgers while the event stream still delivers the ledger events. He does not detail the implementation of those safeguards.

05

在事故中使用入职时的上下文

Fifty consumers needed the ledger. Alongside Vanguard’s three-character application code, the team asked each one for a description and business justification. A trade order might need cash data to meet its obligation; a retirement application might support a required distribution; a 529 application might support a contribution. Those descriptions say what the data enables, not just which application receives it.

AI tools can compile those descriptions into a view of modernized use cases and the business functions the ledger supports. They also make an incident question easier to ask: what happens if a service-level agreement is breached? Yoder contrasts a batch application, where the team may have hours to respond, with a real-time API returning errors to a client, where it may have minutes.

The team was still evaluating how to combine business justifications with observability and log traces to improve response time. The useful link is between an application identifier in an incident and the human consequence of its failure; the integration was presented as ongoing work.

06

向前修复、共享上下文、保持可观测性

AI arrived after the migration had begun, and the teams adopted it as their tools changed. Yoder reports that 90% of cash-program engineers now use AI tools daily for work that includes research, development, and testing. Teams also use multi-agent workflows and shared knowledge bases. In the “team brain” approach, knowledge generated while building features goes into common storage, where a new engineer can bring it into a prompt alongside context available to long-standing teammates.

Maintaining two codebases creates a harder choice when the team discovers an old bug. Yoder recommends fixing it in the modern system and using that improvement as an incentive to migrate. That can make the databases intentionally differ, so the audit process needs a way to allowlist expected discrepancies rather than treat every difference as a migration failure.

The most difficult producers and consumers to onboard, in Yoder’s experience, were those trying to transfer the old design directly. Teams that reconsidered their products could use the modern ledger’s capabilities. His closing advice joins that redesign with practical support: make legacy code queryable, maintain migration docs while people need them, and adopt AI incrementally as tools become available.

Observability remains necessary after the migration. Metrics, alarms, and traces help teams see who produces and consumes data and support the ledger in daily operations. The direct inserts that once appeared without an identifiable producer show why that visibility matters.

要点总结

  • 通过 MCP 暴露遗留代码，让团队能直接向 AI 工具提出调研问题；Yoder 报告反馈循环从七天缩短到 15 分钟。
  • 每日的迁移审计既比较行数，也比较单个属性。由于只在新系统中做的修复而产生的预期差异，需要加入白名单。
  • 一个通用网关给 35 个生产方提供了现代化的写入路径，而可查询的迁移文档减少了团队所报告的支持负担。
  • 实时、批处理和流式使用方各有不同的访问模式；“至少一次”的事件投递让安全的重试处理变得重要。
  • 使用方提供的业务理由可以把一个应用的技术故障与其影响联系起来，而指标、告警和 trace 对日常运营依然必不可少。
