我们让理财规划师自己写智能体

在 Range，规划师把自己的判断变成了可复用的智能体技能。这个方法从熟悉的工作出发，记录下每个决策背后的问题，并让给客户的建议和共享的技能都处在人工审核之下。

01

更快的消息并不能为下一位客户制定方案

Range’s first use of AI was familiar: write a client message, paste it into a chatbot, and ask for clearer or friendlier wording. That saved a few minutes, but the planner still performed the work manually. Matthew Ragone, a certified financial planner who leads AI adoption for Range’s planning team, opens with the constraint that made a different workflow necessary: a planner can only be in one place at a time.

During a call with one client, the planner cannot also build another client’s plan. An agent changes that allocation of time. While Ragone is on the call, it drafts a cash flow analysis or an insurance analysis for a different client. After the call, he opens the draft, reviews it fully, makes corrections, and sends the recommendations. The first draft can now progress without his continuous attention.

The handoff matters because the output is financial advice to real clients in a regulated business. “The agent doesn’t send anything.” Every recommendation passes through a planner before a client sees it. The workflow delegates preparation while keeping the decision to deliver advice with the professional reviewing it.

02

一个技能记录了这家公司如何完成一项工作

Getting to that workflow required more than a polished prompt. Ragone identifies two requirements that solve different problems:

- Work in context. The agent must operate where the work lives, avoiding a separate chat window that requires the planner to copy information back and forth. - Use the firm’s judgment. General financial-planning knowledge does not specify what Range checks first, what it always flags, or how it phrases a particular recommendation.

A skill is the firm’s way of doing one specific piece of work, written down so an agent can follow it. That definition makes the practitioner’s knowledge the main input: the person must know the sequence, the exceptions, and what a good result looks like. Ragone organizes the build process into five steps—pick, teach, pack, test, and ship—none of which required the planners to write code.

03

选择你能判断的工作，再解释这个判断

The first selection criterion is familiarity. Choose a workflow performed every week whose bad drafts are easy for the practitioner to recognize. Ragone advises starting with relatively simple tasks that still require substantial manual effort, rather than reaching immediately for the most complex work. At Range, cash flow analyses and insurance reviews fit that combination.

Recognizing quality is part of the build process itself. If the author cannot judge an output quickly, testing becomes difficult and confidence in the skill has little foundation. Repetition supplies opportunities to use the skill; expertise supplies a way to assess whether it performed the work properly.

Teaching starts with several actual client cases from plans already delivered. Walk through each case from beginning to end and narrate the reasons behind the actions. The pay-stub example makes the distinction concrete: “Next, I open the pay stub” records an action, but leaves out why that document matters, what the planner is looking for, and what discovery would require stopping to ask a question.

That narration turns an observable sequence into guidance for decisions. A list of documents to open cannot, by itself, tell an agent when the available information is insufficient. Explaining what prompts a question captures some of the judgment that makes the result Range’s work rather than generic advice. The talk gives this teaching method without specifying the financial checks performed on the pay stub.

04

把问题和停止点与流程打包在一起

Packaging the skill means writing more than a procedure. Two kinds of instruction govern how the agent should proceed:

- Questions it must ask. Specify what it needs to know before starting and what it should confirm with the planner instead of guessing. - Limits it must respect. Specify actions it must never take independently. At Range, the central example is sending recommendations to a client: the agent drafts, and a planner decides whether the draft needs changes and whether to send it.

These instructions address different failure modes. Required questions interrupt unsupported assumptions before they become part of the analysis. Limits prevent a completed draft from turning automatically into a client communication. The procedure describes how to do the work; the questions and limits describe when human input or a human decision is required.

Ragone compares a skill to a recipe that Claude can follow on command. The useful part of that analogy is the reusable written process. His next step—repeated testing on real cases—also makes clear that writing the recipe does not finish the job.

05

分发前先测试，再给技能指定负责人

Before anyone else uses the skill, run it five to ten times on real cases. The failures to watch for are specific: an edge case the instructions do not cover, a question the agent should have asked, or an answer that sounds confident but is wrong. Each discovery calls for changing the skill, saving it, and running it again. The corrective artifact is the reusable instruction, so the next attempt can benefit from what the author learned.

The cost comparison is distribution. Five to ten trials take effort, but an unfinished skill handed to 15, 20, or 100 colleagues can multiply the cleanup. Those figures illustrate Ragone’s rollout warning; the testing recommendation is a practical starting point, not a demonstrated error-rate guarantee.

Shipping adds two distinct responsibilities:

- Owner. Keep the skill current as internal processes and tax rules change. A skill that was correct in March can be wrong by December even if nobody edits it. - Reviewer. Have someone other than the author check the skill before the team relies on it. Range applies the same independence it expects when reviewing a financial plan: “You don’t grade your own work.”

Skill review and recommendation review operate at different levels. A separate reviewer checks the shared instructions before colleagues depend on them; a planner still reviews each resulting client recommendation before delivery. Maintaining the process does not remove the need to inspect its outputs.

06

工作在扩散，责任也必须随之扩散

Six months into the effort, Ragone reports 24 skills across Range, including 17 in planning. Sales, product, and marketing adopted the approach without a mandate after seeing the planners use it. The skills were written and maintained by the practitioners themselves—planners, marketers, and salespeople.

Practitioner authorship made the instructions more specific. The person with the strongest insurance expertise could build the insurance skill; the tax specialist could build the tax skill. Peer review also had an existing home in the organization. Planners already reviewed one another’s plans, so reviewing one another’s skills extended a familiar professional habit to a new artifact.

The ending shifts attention from whether a model can produce a cash flow draft to who makes that draft useful and dependable inside a firm. Someone must write down the firm’s method, test it before release, and own it once 15 other people depend on it. Ragone presents Range’s experience as one working method and acknowledges that there is little field data yet. The reported adoption shows that practitioners can build and maintain these skills; it does not establish a general solution for checking professional judgment at scale.

要点总结

  • 智能体可以在理财规划师打电话时准备另一位客户的分析，但每一条建议都由规划师审查，交付也由其掌控。
  • 从重复的、相对简单、作者能快速判断质量的工作开始。通过真实案例来教，并解释每个动作为什么重要。
  • 一个有用的技能除了完成任务的步骤之外，还包括必须提出的问题，以及对独立行动的限制。
  • 在分享之前跑五到十个真实案例，纠正遗漏的问题和自信的错误，并指定一位维护负责人和一位独立审查者。
