AI 智能体把 3 天的投资组合构建缩短到几分钟 —— Jack McDonald（Apollo）与 Michael Manapat（Rowspace）

Redding Ridge 用 AI 把零散的信贷数据整合进投资组合构建和交易对账中。更难的工作在于决定该信任哪些信息，并让人对决策负责。

Jack McDonald 在台上做开场介绍，作为本文的讲者照片。

01

信贷决策始于零散的数据

A portfolio manager at Redding Ridge works with loan information scattered across private documents and systems while making decisions in an active market. Jack McDonald, an Apollo principal and Redding Ridge’s COO, says the structured credit and collateralized loan obligation (CLO) manager oversees about $50 billion in assets and receives thousands of investment data points daily.

Michael Manapat, co-founder of Rowspace, describes its platform as using agents to reconcile information across systems. The immediate challenge is to make the data accurate, consistent and usable. That requires understanding Redding Ridge’s calculations and which source should supply each piece of information.

02

几分钟内搭建一个模拟投资组合

A CLO portfolio must satisfy specific tests and follow a defined waterfall for distributing money. Those rules live in lengthy indentures and other legal agreements. Before using Rowspace, a portfolio manager reviewed the documents, built Excel models and assembled a mock portfolio of roughly 200 to 400 individual credit names. McDonald says that manual work typically took two to three days.

The newer workflow brings together information from document libraries, trade and compliance systems, and real-time pricing. McDonald says it can produce a portfolio in minutes, at roughly 80–90% completion, leaving the manager to apply judgment and refine it. These are his operational estimates; the discussion does not provide an accuracy measurement or a controlled comparison.

03

把每周的对账变成一份可重复的报告

Redding Ridge also uses bank financing to buy loans. Its team historically produced a weekly recap of purchases and sales by checking bank accounts and statements to see where trades had actually settled, reconciling the activity, and emailing management. McDonald says the process consumed many hours a week across two or three people.

The same report is now represented in Rowspace and can be recreated in about a minute, according to McDonald. The team checks the automated output daily to see whether it looks right. The change removes repeated manual assembly while retaining a review of the result.

04

在依赖答案之前先选对数据源

A faster report matters only if its inputs support trading and portfolio decisions. McDonald finds general-purpose models useful for research and getting up to speed on an investment, but describes Redding Ridge’s need as owning its data and building a trusted platform around it.

One data point might appear in 20 sources, with only one right for the task. Rowspace works alongside portfolio management and finance staff to understand calculations, identify the correct information and override competing sources. Redding Ridge’s team validates the information it expects to rely on.

Those sources include trade order management and performance systems, Bloomberg and pricing indexes. Previously, staff often had to lift pieces from separate systems and join them manually; Rowspace automates that aggregation. The discussion describes the source-selection work and human validation, but does not specify the implementation used to resolve each conflict.

05

从例行工作到一个可能的竞争优势

Manapat describes Apollo’s early interest in an AI-native platform rather than isolated tools. McDonald connects that approach to an openness to changing workflows, improving staff capabilities and replacing manual processes so people have more time to think.

McDonald sees adoption across asset management, investment banking and large financial institutions as early. Research aggregation and model creation already have value, and he expects some functions may need less junior support over time. At Redding Ridge, the present focus is more immediate: make routine tasks efficient and free capacity for a long list of other projects.

Could AI become as commonplace as Excel, or provide a lasting edge? McDonald expects an advantage when a firm combines good execution processes with information its team trusts: available liquidity, current pricing and value. In his view, better information can help firms move ahead, especially as private markets evolve. A misinformed decision, meanwhile, can carry consequences through the enterprise to investors. The competitive advantage is his forecast, not a measured result presented in the talk.

06

和懂工作流的人一起构建

McDonald’s near-term goal is confidence in information sent to Redding Ridge’s more than 400 global investors without spending hours manually checking it. He also wants technology to become an earlier consideration when teams encounter a problem: ask why a process exists, how it works and whether it should be improved or changed.

For builders, the warning is that an unusual step in a financial workflow may be there for a reason. McDonald credits Rowspace’s progress against its roadmap to spending time almost every day with the people doing the work. Sitting with front-office professionals helps a builder learn the calculations, exceptions and information flows that a useful tool must accommodate.

要点总结

  • McDonald 称，一个包含 200–400 个标的的模拟 CLO 投资组合，过去在 Excel 中要花两到三天，现在几分钟就能准备好，供经理进一步完善。
  • 自动化的交易对账大约一分钟就能重建一份报告，团队每天检查它的输出。
  • 可信的金融数据需要解决相互冲突的来源，并与使用这些数据的人一起验证计算。
  • 在例行任务上节省的时间，可以让团队有余地质疑并改进流程本身。
